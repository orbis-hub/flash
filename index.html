orbis · flash an e-ink display

puts the orbis firmware on your board straight from the browser. no ide, no drivers. needs chrome or edge (web serial) and a usb data cable.

1 · pick your board

2 · connect & flash
your browser has no web serial. use chrome or edge on a desktop. web serial needs https or localhost. version: …

plug the board in via usb first. if it does not show up in the port list, hold BOOT while pressing RST once (or hold BOOT while plugging in) to enter download mode. inkplate and lilygo boards usually need no button dance.

3 · pair it with your hub
  1. in orbis go to e-ink in the sidebar → + display, pick the same board, assign a dashboard. open token & setup.
  2. after flashing the board starts a wifi called orbis-eink. connect to it with your phone or laptop; a page opens (or visit 192.168.4.1).
  3. choose your wifi, paste hub url, display id and token from step 1. save.
  4. the display shows your dashboard within a minute and refreshes every few minutes (interval is set on the hub). hold BOOT while powering on to reopen the setup wifi.
other boards
any esp32 + e-paper combo gxepd2 knows works: copy an env in platformio.ini, set the panel class and size, build with platformio. touch overlays with gt911 or ft6x36 controllers are detected automatically when you set the i2c pins. pull requests with new board envs are welcome.
